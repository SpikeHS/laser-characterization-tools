# Roadmap

## Available in 0.1.0
- Local spectral-file import, current/bias maps, spectrum inspection and JSON export.
- LIV metrics with explicit fit bounds, provenance and plots.
- Synthetic examples, regression tests, packaging and Windows/Linux CI.

## Planned
- Link spectral and LIV measurements by device ID, current, temperature and mode.
- Record missing/mismatched conditions rather than infer unsupported associations.
- Support pulse/average measurement metadata and independently tested corrections.
- Import additional instrument formats through documented adapters.
- Connect growth/sample/process/device identifiers to PL and beam-profile results.
- Define and evaluate predictive baselines when traceable datasets are sufficient.

These are future milestones, not released features or adoption claims.
